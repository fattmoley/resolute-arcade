The golden ball

One ball in the machine is gold. Fish it out and crack it open: once a week there is a golden ticket inside, which puts you and a friend on the list for any ReSolute night. The rest of the time it holds something stranger.

Not valid for events run with another promoter.

Official rules
  1. No purchase necessary. The Flyer Machine is free to play. Buying anything does not improve your chances.
  2. Who runs it. The golden ticket giveaway is run by ReSolute, New York. Apple is not a sponsor and is not involved in any way.
  3. Who can play. Anyone 18 or older. To use a ticket you must also meet the age and entry rules of the event you attend.
  4. How to win. Pull the gold ball out of the machine and open it. Some of the time it holds a golden ticket, chosen at random. At most one ticket is given out in any seven-day period, so your odds depend on how many people open the gold ball that week.
  5. Claiming. If you win, enter your name and email on the screen that appears. A ticket that is not claimed there and then expires and goes back in the machine.
  6. The prize. One golden ticket: entry for you and one friend to one ReSolute night of your choice within 365 days of winning. Not valid for events run with another promoter. Entry is subject to venue capacity and door policy. No cash value, not for resale, not transferable.
  7. Your details. We use your name and email only to put you on the list and send you the ticket.
  8. Small print. Void where prohibited. ReSolute may change or end the giveaway at any time. Tickets already claimed stay valid.
  9. Questions. hello@resolutenyc.com
